FoodLens AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What the app does

FoodLens AI helps you find recipes based on ingredients you have, using photos or manual entry.

Data we handle

  • Photos you choose to analyse. Sent to our server and an AI provider (Google Gemini) only after you tap “Use this photo”, to identify ingredients. Photos are not shared publicly and are not kept after analysis.
  • Preferences (diet, allergies, cuisines), favorites, kitchen items, shopping list and history: stored on your device; if you use an account they may be stored on our servers (Supabase).
  • Usage metadata (e.g. number of scans, request status) to enforce daily limits and manage costs.
  • Analytics and diagnostics (app events, crash information) via Firebase, if enabled.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob shows ads and may use your advertising ID, subject to your consent choices. Diet and allergy information is never used for ad targeting.

Your choices

Change ad consent in Profile → Ad privacy choices. Delete your data in Profile → Delete all my data, which also removes server-side data for your account. You may also email us to request deletion.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Food safety

Allergen and nutrition information is estimated automatically and may be wrong. Always check labels.
