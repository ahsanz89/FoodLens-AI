# FoodLens AI — Brand Guide

**Name:** FoodLens AI · **Tagline:** Cook with what you already have
**Promise:** Point your camera at your ingredients; get recipes you can cook right now, ranked by how much you already have.
**Personality:** Warm, practical, trustworthy, a little playful. Never preachy about food or health.

## Logo
A pan seen from above: the **white ring is the lens**, the **orange yolk is the ingredient it found**, three steam lines show it is cooking. Files: `brand/logo-mark.svg` (app icon, rounded square), `brand/logo-lockup.svg` (horizontal). Keep clear space equal to the ring's thickness; never recolor the yolk or stretch the mark.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Lens Green (primary) | `#2E8B57` | buttons, icon, headings accent |
| Deep Forest | `#0B4D2E` | store graphics, dark surfaces |
| Yolk Orange (accent) | `#FF9F43` | highlights, warnings, partial matches |
| Cream | `#FAFAF7` | app background |
| Ink | `#16211B` | body text |
| Mint | `#E9F6EE` | chips, tinted cards |

## Typography
Poppins (Bold/ExtraBold for headlines, Medium for body) in marketing; system font in-app for performance and accessibility.

## Voice
Short, friendly, action-first ("Scan ingredients", "You can cook now"). Always say "you confirm what the AI found"; never claim medical or allergy guarantees.

## Store assets
`assets/store/` (icon 512, feature graphic) and `store/screenshots/` (1080×1920).
